# Creditor payments move the bank; my share first, then fronting

Supersedes the first bullet of ADR 0003 ("paying a creditor installment touches neither bank nor ledger"). Display-only creditor payments forced the user to record every payment twice, so each pay action (installment, expense or full debt) now requires a Paid from Bank/Cash account and posts one transaction in the same request: `Dr Creditor payments` (an Expense account) `/ Cr Bank`. A creditor installment is the full purchase installment, while split shares accrue `Dr Receivable / Cr CreditorPayable` at the due month, so booking the whole payment as expense would overstate my spending by the parties' shares and never clear `CreditorPayable`. A payment therefore fills my share first and only the remainder debits `CreditorPayable` (I am fronting; the party still owes me). Pro-rata allocation was rejected because party share payments already reduce the same installment, so a pro-rata split could pay a share twice. A party share payment keeps its settlement (money in from the party) and adds a second transaction for the same amount out to the creditor, so the bank nets to zero. A synchronous ledger call was chosen over an outbox event, matching back-dated card plans; undo-last reverses the stored transaction, and older display-only payments stay display-only.
