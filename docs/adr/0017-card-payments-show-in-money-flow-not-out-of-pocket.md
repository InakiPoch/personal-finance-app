# Card payments show in Money Flow but are not Out of pocket

Card bill and card installment payments already post `Dr CardLiability / Cr Bank`, but Money Flow hid them, so the user re-recorded each one as a debit expense — crediting the bank twice and counting the spending twice. Money Flow now shows these postings as outcomes (it is the list of money leaving and entering Bank/Cash), while Out of pocket keeps excluding them, because card spending is deliberately not Out of pocket. This splits Money Flow's outcome filter from the monthly-expenses filter that ADR 0012 made equal. No new event or second posting is added: the existing transaction is the record. New card payments carry a description; older ones fall back to the card name derived from the liability account, since Financing never stored which statement or installment a payment transaction belongs to and matching by timestamp would be fragile.
